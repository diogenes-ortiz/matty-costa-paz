Bio

Matty Costa Paz is an interior designer specialising in interior architecture, creating spaces for the emotion that beauty brings, for sharing moments and living a life well lived, for over thirty years.

Her architectural language is sophisticated, timeless and refined, conceived for each client, understanding their dreams, aspirations and desires.

As a designer she places great importance on the harmony between her interiors and their surroundings, and on the sustainability of every project.

Based in Buenos Aires with projects around the world, and recipient of multiple awards, her work has been published in AD Italia, Casa Vogue, Architectural Digest and Elle Decor, among other international publications.

A constant traveller, year after year she seeks inspiration in different places around the world, experiences that find their way into her projects.

She is a member of the Design Leadership Network.

Honorary member of DINA (Diseñadores Nacionales Asociados), she served for nearly a decade on the executive board of DArA, where she left her mark on four editions of the international DArA ID congress.
