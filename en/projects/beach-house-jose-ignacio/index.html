Club de Mar, José Ignacio

Club de Mar, José Ignacio project details

Location
José Ignacio, Uruguay
Completed
2020
Scope
Architecture, interior and landscape design
Published in
Elle Argentina

The studio was brought in to complete a beachfront house in José Ignacio. The original turnkey project left a lot undefined before it could become a tailored place with its own identity.

Everything was rethought, from the façade to the landscaping, and new spaces and uses emerged along the way. Rooms were designed to be lived in both summer and winter, with a focus on the privacy of the people who live there.

The project draws on Moroccan architecture: the predominance of white, natural materials, the central reflecting pool, stone and timber lattices on the openings. Inside, a modern art collection sits alongside furniture and objects found on long searches.
