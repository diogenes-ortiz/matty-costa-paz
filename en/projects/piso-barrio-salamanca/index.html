Barrio de Salamanca

Barrio de Salamanca project details

Location
Madrid, Spain
Completed
2023
Scope
Interior design
Area
Almost 300 m²
Photography
Belén Imaz
Published in
Elle Decor España, Casa Vogue Brasil

The owner, from Argentina, was starting a new chapter in Madrid and once again entrusted her home to Matty Costa Paz: twenty years earlier they had done her first project together, a penthouse in Palermo Chico.

The apartment had everything going for it: natural wood floors, very high ceilings, balconies that flood the living room with light and a courtyard that brings silence to the private areas. The challenge was to give it its own character while respecting and enhancing the classic spirit of the building.

The design proposes an open plan, with visual continuity between the entrance, living room, dining room and an intimate sitting room set behind a bronze aluminium and glass partition. The idea was to create connections, not divisions. Plaster mouldings in the style of the building’s period were added against the white walls, bringing rhythm and quality to each room.

The project was completed in eight months and several trips. The result is a serene retreat, a blank page for a new chapter.
