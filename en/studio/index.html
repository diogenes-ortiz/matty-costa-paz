Studio

I am an interior designer, and for over 30 years I have worked on residential and corporate projects in Buenos Aires, José Ignacio, Paris and Madrid.

I am self-taught: when I finished school, interior design was not yet a degree. A visit to Casa FOA in 1985 settled it for me, and for more than 25 years I worked on projects alongside major architecture firms.

I am known for integrating interior design with architecture. I always work as a team with the client, the architecture studio and the builder, from the first layout to the last detail.

I travel all year round in search of new design and take part in congresses around the world. I share what I learn in specialised publications and in talks at universities and companies.

Philosophy

  1. 01
